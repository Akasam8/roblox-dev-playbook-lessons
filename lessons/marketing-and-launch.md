## Effort

- **Give marketing as much effort as the game.** Many devs spend ages on loops, looks and sound but no time researching how they'll get players in. Plan marketing early. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7702655479822011678), 2026-10-31

## Thumbnails and icons

- **Reverse-engineer winning thumbnails.** Study thumbnails and icons of high-CCU games in your genre, ask AI what makes them convert (curiosity gap, contrast, faces/reactions, one clear subject) and to write a prompt in that style, then generate several original options and rank them. The concept matters more than the tool: AI-made thumbnails can match Blender ones. ## Positioning

- **Have AI critique your pitch as a skeptical prospect.** Give the AI your page or offer and ask it to role-play a potential customer listing the first things that would make them hesitate, plus how to fix each; package what you sell as one clear, no-brainer offer rather than a bare product. ## Ads purpose

- **Treat early ads as buying data, not players.** Early ad spend may bring few concurrent players, but it gives you funnel and retention data to improve the game; be patient and act on that data instead of judging ads by instant CCU. (x3) — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7690369952288754974), 2026-09-28; @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7691880729000594719), 2026-10-02; @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7692243083236216094), 2026-10-03
- **Soft launch ads first, then go full budget.** Decide your total ad budget up front, then start with a small spend to collect data and fix the major problems (treat paid traffic as paid playtesters feeding your funnels), and only run the full daily budget once you're comfortable. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7706735080567295263), 2026-11-11

## Ad budget

- **Set aside a real ad budget, roughly $10-20 a day.** Games rarely take off without ads unless you already have a social audience. Budget around $10-20/day (or plan total Robux and campaign length first), fund it from a job or other income, and accept that some spend won't pay back. Budget for everything else too: AI subscriptions, freelance scripters/builders/animators, video makers and monetisation all cost money. ## Ad creatives

- **Test 3 thumbnails, keep those above 3% CTR.** Launch a campaign with three thumbnails in different styles, let it run about 24 hours to exit learning, then cut anything under about 3% CTR and remake the losers in the winning style. One dev reached 5.3% CTR on plays ads and about 4% on engagement ads after repeated testing. ## Ad types

- **Use engagement ads to reach the highly engaged player threshold.** A highly engaged player (HEP) is 16+, age-verified, has account history and has spent Robux on Roblox in the last 60 days, not just someone with long playtime. Plays ads reach everyone and bring volume, while engagement ads bring fewer but qualifying players; one dev hit 250 HEP for about $160 (1.2M impressions). *(Time-sensitive: check Roblox's current rules.)* (x5) — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7693737527059942686), 2026-10-07; @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7694115044769926431), 2026-10-08; @isaacrocreate, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@isaacrocreate/video/7699679489525026069), 2026-10-23; @isaacrocreate, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@isaacrocreate/video/7701345683445402900), 2026-10-27; @isaacrocreate, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@isaacrocreate/video/7704083768302144789), 2026-11-03

## Experience page

- **Make the experience page as good as the game.** Players judge the page before the gameplay. Show real gameplay, the core loop and the most fun moments with SFX in the video; research search terms for the title, description and tags; and simulate a kid choosing between your thumbnail and 20 competitors. A weak page is a common cause of high CTR but low plays. ## Social media

- **Post dev clips daily and pay small recommendation creators.** Before or alongside paid ads, post your game on TikTok and other socials consistently, reply in comments (without spamming), and talk about it everywhere; it also attracts backers. Paying creators who post Roblox game recommendations about $20-50, after checking their analytics, is a cheap boost. ## Reveals

- **Hold back full gameplay reveals until near launch.** Showing all the mechanics too early lets others copy the idea quickly with AI; tease pieces, then post gameplay showcases once the game is close to final so you can follow up with content immediately. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7687807546677136670), 2026-09-21
