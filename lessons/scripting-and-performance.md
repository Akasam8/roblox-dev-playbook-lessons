## Optimisation

- **Have Claude find your heaviest models.** Ask the agent to list the ~10 most performance-heavy models in the place and suggest optimisations that keep their look (part count, collisions, mesh/texture size, CastShadow). — @momentumcash, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7705148037508762910), 2026-11-06

## Security

- **AI-built games are easy to hack; validate on server.** A creator vibe-coded a working aimbot with fly and wall-shooting for someone else's AI-made game, showing such games often trust the client; make sure your server validates movement, hits and damage rather than trusting client input. — @momentumcash, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7671170825797569823), 2026-08-07

## Device performance

- **Offer low/medium/high graphics settings for mobile.** Add an in-game graphics quality option (auto, low, medium, high) where low disables reflections and heavy lighting, so mobile players get smooth performance while PC players keep full visuals. — @momentumcash, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7702163938602994975), 2026-10-29
