## Launch timing

- **Polish the first impression, but don't wait for perfect.** A new game's first players decide whether it grows, so the first session must look, feel and sound good. But once the core loop works and you have done 4-5 playtests, release, and use public funnels and ad data to find what to fix before spending your main marketing budget. (x2) — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7688203825106242847), 2026-09-22; @isaacrocreate, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@isaacrocreate/video/7698935575545515284), 2026-10-21

## Iterate or kill

- **Rework a weak launch with data, or drop a dead idea.** If a launch underperforms, use funnels and player feedback to rework it rather than abandoning it immediately. But if CCU keeps falling and the dislike ratio grows despite fixes, take the loss and move on to a new idea. (x2) — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7690771417947753759), 2026-09-29; @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7698565887749524767), 2026-10-20

## Post-launch

- **Keep updating a game that's making money.** Don't cash in and abandon a successful game; regular updates keep players around and spending more. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7697455617312656671), 2026-10-17
