## Shop and upgrades

- **Simulate a kid browsing your shop to find ignored items.** Identify which shop items look boring, confusing or get skipped, and which upgrades feel pointless; fix both the presentation and the actual usefulness of the item rather than just the price. ## Competitor research

- **Study how top games monetise, then pick buildable features.** Have an agent analyse the monetisation of hit games like Blox Fruits and Steal a Brainrot, let several agents debate which approach fits your game, and come out with three specific features you can actually build. — @momentumcash, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7704374814928145695), 2026-11-04

## Fair monetisation

- **Never paywall what the core loop needs.** Core mechanics such as sprint or jump must be free; passes should add extra value (accessories, bonus items) on top of a complete free game, which keeps players long enough to spend. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7698567051547364638), 2026-10-20

## Product ideas

- **Turn playtester wishes into starter packs and boosts.** Watch full playthroughs and note anything testers say takes too long or they wish they had earlier; offer those as optional speed-ups, starter packs or deals while keeping the game fair to play for free. — @isaacrocreate, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@isaacrocreate/video/7702538128631827733), 2026-10-30
