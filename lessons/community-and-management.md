## Rules and IP

- **Don't build on other people's IP.** Roblox is taking down AI-ported or copied games (e.g. CS:GO clones, console emulators) and games using anime or brand names without commercial rights. Check IP before using any name or idea. (x2) — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7702283329499876638), 2026-10-30; @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7702655401120075039), 2026-10-31

## Caution

- **Be wary of "promoters" and paid helpers.** Many people selling promotion, thumbnails or "help blowing up" your game just want your money, and theft is common. Research anyone first and expect most games not to be profitable. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7705614232137583903), 2026-11-08

## Feedback mining

- **Let AI mine player feedback for recurring pain points.** Feed reviews, comments, Discord messages and support tickets to an AI and ask for repeated problems, the outcomes players care about, the exact words they use and their objections, then reuse that language in your game description and marketing. — @momentumcash, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7693082937251663134), 2026-10-05

## Community events

- **Run owner events and giveaways for your community.** Once you have players and followers, host server-wide owner events, admin-abuse sessions and giveaways; looking after your community is what keeps a game alive. — @dsrstudiorblx, [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@dsrstudiorblx/video/7689661828846144799), 2026-09-26

## Business

- **Reject too-good buyout offers and save for taxes.** Be wary of studios offering quick money for your game; scams are common, so don't rush. Roblox earnings are taxable at any age, so set money aside and get a parent or trusted adult to help sort it out.
