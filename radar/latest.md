## Idea signals (what to remix, not copy)

1. An absurd setting plus a boring job: sweep the MOON, run a MOTEL, throw food to ANIMALS. The joke is the hook.
2. One input, visible progression: an arc, a distance, zones or biomes, and an income per second number that grows.
3. A rule as the title (DONT, Do Not, Can You) gives an instant challenge with zero explanation.
4. Ragdoll should be a feature, not the game: put it inside a job or goal (deliveries, a stunt test, rescuing someone) rather than an empty sandbox.
5. Seasonal window: Christmas jobs (wrapping, delivering, decorating) are open right now and work as cozy sims.

## Icon patterns (the square tile)

One subject filling 70% or more of the tile, close up, with a strong face emotion: the screaming ice face (Hyper Realistic Ragdoll), the angry psycho close-up, the barber's grin.
Bright sky-blue or grass-green background for casual and sim games (Throw Food, Build a Bridge, Survive Your Ideas). Dark and moody for horror (Do Not Stop, Ditch Hotel, 13 Days).
Text is 1 to 3 huge words with thick outlines, or none: BEARD SEASON!, TSUNAMI, NAME AN ANIMAL, ADOPT ME!
A number or progress hook on the tile: $997,151,167/s on Steal a Brainrot, a red X with 67% on Draw the Other Half.
A meme animal or Roblox noob face in the middle of the action: doge sweeping, noob holding an egg, noob riding a wave.
A NEW! badge in the corner (Sweep The Moon).
Minimal single objects (the MM2 knife, the RIVALS red glove, the RoChess knight) only work for already famous games or when the object is the whole game.

## In-game thumbnail patterns (the 16:9 images after clicking)

Show the core loop as a diagram. Throw Food For Animals draws a yellow throw arc from the player to a Phoenix across 4 biome blocks, with PHOENIX $68,543B/s in rainbow text. That's the loop, the progression and the reward in one image.
The big reward number in rainbow or green outlined text: TREX EGG $89,121Sp/s (Steal An Egg), with a red curved arrow and red !! marks pointing at the prize.
A control hint: Sweep The Moon shows a mouse-click SWEEP button, so you know the input before you join.
A premium cinematic render for horror and driving: Do Not Stop uses a realistic snowy scene, a monster lunging at a jumping car, a bold lowercase logo and a small A DRIVING GAME subtitle.
A NEW! badge on the first thumbnail.
